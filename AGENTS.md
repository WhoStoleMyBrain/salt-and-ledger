# Repository guidance

Salt & Ledger is a Unity 6 maritime economic strategy project. Task 2.0 adds Expanding Economy v3: exact 10,000-subunit goods/coins, proportional paid retail, expandable private industries, Silver/public Mint and finite external contracts, a three-ship player fleet, reference Company Value and complete local saves. Task 2.1 adds the map-first Grand Commission objective draft and Journal. Task 2.2 adds Harbor and independently versioned shared company town stock for new v3 runs, completion acknowledgment and read-only diagnostic exports. Task 2.3 adds bounded ordered multi-stop cargo itineraries and the Fleet route planner for new v3 pooled runs. Classic, seeded-v1, Living Cities-v2 and historical scenarios/reports/saves remain regression fixtures. Read `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md`, `docs/MECHANICS.md`, `docs/LIVING_CITIES.md`, `docs/EXPANDING_ECONOMY.md`, `docs/GRAND_COMMISSION.md`, `docs/HARBOR.md` and `docs/AUTOMATION.md` before changing boundaries or behavior.

- Keep `src/SaltAndLedger.Simulation` independent of Unity, presentation, file-system policy, and documentation generation. Preserve `netstandard2.1` and C# 8 compatibility.
- Prefer small concrete types and explicit state transitions. Do not add speculative service layers, plugin systems, or interfaces for deferred features.
- Keep content in `data/content.json`; keep its structural contract in `data/content.schema.json`. Stable IDs are globally unique and must not depend on display names. Keep Python and C# semantic validation aligned.
- Use finite nonnegative integer inventory quantities and checked arithmetic. A failed production command must leave inventory unchanged. Preserve deterministic behavior; do not introduce wall-clock time or unseeded randomness into the simulation.
- Keep world operations atomic: exceptional failure must preserve all inventories, cash balances, controls, and time, including earlier staged attempts. Runtime instances reference immutable canonical definitions. Legacy steps validate commands first, apply controls, then trades in declared order, then production by ascending priority and ordinal instance ID. Expected stock/funds shortages are completed outcomes; do not retain unbounded history inside the world.
- Use nonnegative `long` cash and positive `long` prices with checked public numeric bounds. Trading conserves goods and combined cash. The existing location inventory is market stock shared with production; trader stock stays separate. Preserve the fixed-price baseline and keep experimental pricing parameters in runtime configuration. Only explicit extraction creates goods. Legacy consumption is unpaid; living household retail pays the market and municipal own-use pays nobody. Never replenish cash or silently repair stock.
- Preserve the single-location API. `MaritimeWorldState` owns exactly two independent ports, one trader cash account, one ship cargo inventory, and one global tick; never synchronize separate `WorldState` instances. Reuse the shared trade calculator and existing pricing/production operations. Cargo is the maritime trader's only goods inventory; all items occupy one capacity unit.
- Legacy `MaritimeWorldState` steps validate first, then apply controls, docked trades in declared order, departure, production by ordinal port ID and existing building order, and one travel advance before a single atomic commit. Its departure consumes one travel tick; arrival allows trading on the next step. Remote previews are hypothetical, never execution permission. Capacity shortage is ordinary rejection; illegal ship/port actions are exceptional and roll back every port, account, control, ship state, and tick.
- Keep route policies immutable and evaluation stateless over committed snapshots. The legacy maritime path retains one automated trade at most per tick and host-owned enablement. Regional actors store their immutable policy and enablement in authoritative snapshots; historical routes run at most four dockside actions per atomic chain, with a fresh evaluation/preview after each staged action. New itineraries use their separately versioned eight-operation daily budget below. Preserve per-order expenditure caps, reserves, unsold cargo, explicit wait/pause reasons, and no mixed manual commands for an enabled actor.
- Keep `RegionalWorldState` as one owner of all ports, company accounts, ship cargo, private stock, schedules, challenge progress, day, and revision. Instant commands never advance the day; changed commits increment revision and no-op/rejected commands retain snapshot identity. Reject stale decisions/previews within the same day. Daily order is actor priority/ordinal ID, private procurement, public production by port/priority/ID, private production, private sales, consumption by port/kind/rule ID (legacy retains ordinal rule order), living welfare/population and next-day quote parameters, voyages, due construction completion, then challenge evaluation; exceptional failure rolls back everything. Departure spends no day; committed arrival permits immediate dockside trading. Legacy timing must not change.
- Keep `EnterpriseScenario` shared by Unity and new headless experiments; preserve `ArchipelagoScenario` and `LegacyContent.Timber` for historical regression fixtures. Route distances/speeds are integers, independent of map rendering. Extraction and transformation are explicit canonical recipe kinds; intervals, stock ceilings, and consumption belong to runtime configuration. Shortages/disabled/output-limited facilities remain due without catch-up batches; toggles preserve deadlines. Record source, transformation, sink, and transport flows separately.
- Company cash is authoritative; ship cash is a compatibility projection and must not be counted twice. Explicit company configuration requires zero ship InitialCash. Private factory stock never aliases public stock. Construction pays the local market, starts empty, and schedules the first batch one interval after becoming operational. Ship/storage transfers require matching ownership and the actual dock, respect both capacities, and move no cash or time. Remote management may trade locally but cannot move goods between ports.
- Factory recipes remain fixed canonical references. Procurement/sales policies use shared exact pricing, marginal limits, reserves, and bounded quantity searches, with at most one transaction per configured good per daily phase. Do not fabricate cost basis or label sale revenue/unsold output as profit. The Coastal challenge counts signed net target-market sales minus purchases for its owner; all challenge kinds evaluate after atomic operations and freeze economic mutation on success/failure/abandonment. Sandbox remains unlimited.
- Historical costs use per-store FIFO arrival order and preserved acquisition intervals: slice cost is floor(C*end/Q)-floor(C*start/Q). Transfers move original slices without cash or new basis; production consumes gross recipe inputs and allocates exact material cost across ordinal output units. Starter grants have explicit provenance and zero paid basis. Construction is separate capital expenditure. Checked aggregates, lots, route stages and challenge statistics share the world commit/rollback. Snapshots retain open lots and totals; results carry only committed operation events, and the host journal is bounded.
- Industrial policies have two ports and explicit acquire/deliver stages for each direction. Use the shared trade, transfer and departure paths; no remote loading or hidden replenishment. Same-policy application/enablement preserves stage. Replacing a policy requires an empty ship docked at a configured port. Mixed incompatible cargo pauses intact; bound same-day actions to four.
- RunScenario chooses exactly one immutable charter and optional fixed challenge. Starter factories/inventories are deterministic endowments, never fake purchases. Preserve Coastal net-delivery protection; Regional Relief counts actual scheduled world consumption; Enterprise Ambition requires private output plus attributed gross margin. Success/failure/abandonment freeze mutations. Score through RunResults with bounded documented components, compared within challenge/charter, never an unqualified wealth ranking.
- Keep `SeededArchipelago` version 1 pure and bounded: signed int32 seed, controlled integer sequence, four separated jittered slots, six integer ceiling-square-root distances. Seed geography must not change economic roles, initial stocks/accounts, recipes, speeds or deadlines. Keep Classic's authored positions and exact distances; legacy scenario APIs and golden reports remain unchanged.
- Living Cities is an explicit separate v2 mode. Keep `LivingCityGeneration` pure: unchanged v1 geography plus a domain-separated 11-draw economic role permutation and bounded population/liquidity bands. Roles must retain complementary production chains; generated eligibility is enforced by authoritative blueprints. Do not reroll, retune or repair worlds after inspecting strategy outcomes. Identity includes the living pricing mode; adapted charters change locations only, preserving assets and operational grants.
- Living household cash belongs once to each town, separately from its market. Check combined markets+households+companies cash on every commit. Successful public/private batches pay configured wages from the actual employer; payroll shortage prevents the whole batch. Private wages are expensed separately: FIFO remains material-only, and `OperatingSurplusAfterWages` subtracts all paid wages from realized material margin, including wages for held output. Never call that full net profit or capitalize labor silently.
- Living retail uses exact shared marginal totals and bounded affordable quantities, with essentials before luxuries and separate missing-stock/affordability outcomes. Municipal Planks are unpaid own-use. Welfare retains at most six food observations, updates after consumption, and reviews bounded population changes before voyages/construction/challenge evaluation. Coverage parameters are immutable for an entire day; rederive only at initialization/end-of-day from finite purchasing power, never after each instant trade.
- City Recovery measures six actual paid-food opportunities against the larger current/initial-population demand denominator, retained initial population, configured prosperity, company solvency and deadline. Deliveries alone do not count. Public/NPC contributions are regional welfare, not personal attribution. Keep terminal freeze, exact retry and Ascension 0/1 timing intact.
- `RunIdentity` retains challenge, charter, mode, nullable seed, generator version, difficulty, draft and independently versioned company storage and itinerary rules. Retry recreates exactly that identity and clears host state; reroll is explicit host-only seed selection followed by preview/start confirmation. Pooled comparison groups append `/storage-v2`; itinerary version 1 additionally appends `/itinerary-v1`, without changing geography, economic generation or draft signatures. Preserve existing legacy identity groups and old report grouping for identity-null snapshots.
- Grand Commission is v3-only, with independent mission draft version 1 and four salted category streams offering three of four authored templates each. Resolve one offered template per round into immutable typed parameters and a signature in run identity; choices never change economic generation. Victory requires all four current goals and 100 company coins by day 180, with exact-deadline success precedence. Keep prior v3 challenge formulas and comparison groups unchanged.
- Commission history is bounded authoritative state: at most three owned ships with four first-visit dates each, and 28 town/good purchase/sale balances. Credit only actual initial docks, arrivals, commissioned docks and successful player-company ship/warehouse market trades; stage everything atomically. Net sales can reverse on buyback. Derive funded production, welfare, public flows and value from existing snapshots; A New Industry requires funded batches in the same operational Mill/Bakery pair in distinct towns. Distinguish cumulative accomplishments from reversible conditions; card percentage is its minimum component, and partial score is floor(500 times the four percentages' sum divided by 400).
- Old pooled v3 saves with itinerary version 0 retain schema 3 with explicit storage identity and one validated stockpile/FIFO owner per company/town. New v3 pooled itinerary-version-1 identities use schema 4 even before assigning a route. Legacy drafted saves retain schema 2; other valid legacy runs retain schema 1, with unchanged fields, fingerprints and semantics. Absent storage rules mean Legacy and absent itinerary version means 0; never consolidate or convert old routes silently. Reject mismatched versions, worlds, choices, impossible history or duplicate physical/FIFO holdings without replacing the active host. Retry preserves the original commission, storage and itinerary identity. Reveal the full map before drafting; setup selection is passive, Journal pauses playback and the map HUD stays compact. Do not add deferred draft systems or UI-only progress counters.
- Cargo itineraries are immutable version-1 player policies in explicit v3 pooled runs: 2–4 unique connected port stops, 1–8 commands per stop, at most 24 total. Buy/Sell/Load/Unload affect only the named good and preserve other mixed cargo. Maximum/minimum are positive whole-cargo quantities; each command executes its largest qualifying quantity once per visit, then advances even if partial. Below minimum, Skip advances without payment or movement; Wait retains that exact command. Use shared exact prices, ordinary dock/ownership transfers and FIFO; never repeat completed orders or discard cargo.
- Keep itinerary stop/action/cycle, last decision/reason/quantity and OperationsDay/Used inside the authoritative ship snapshot and atomic working copy. At most eight operations per ship per day include successful orders, skips and departure; repeated calls and save/load cannot reset it. Departure follows all stop commands, uses real voyage days and advances cycle only when leaving the last listed stop. Stable actor order and all later daily phases remain unchanged. Exceptional failure rolls back progress/budget together with cash, stores, FIFO and voyages.
- Fleet planner drafts are passive. Replacing an itinerary requires manual control, an actual included dock, no partially executed old stop and no itinerary operations already performed today. Equivalent reapplication preserves progress/budget; viewing and editing need no empty hold. Pausing retains route/cargo/voyage; enabling requires the exact next stop or matching incoming leg. Market, industrial and itinerary policies are mutually exclusive. Schema 4 strictly validates all typed commands, cursor/budget, ownership, required stockpiles and enabled dock/voyage consistency; mid-stop and mid-voyage restoration resumes exact next work. NPC policies and historical reports remain unchanged. The role-aware BreadLoop preset is a draft, never construction, stock or funding.
- `CompanyStorageRules.Legacy = 1` keeps per-facility stock. New v3 host starts explicitly choose `Pooled = 2`; existing API defaults stay Legacy for historical fixtures. Pooled stock uses `company:<owner>:<port>` as its sole material/FIFO store. Facilities retain individual policy, lifecycle, batches, wages and consumed/produced counters, with empty local inventories and a shared-stockpile reference. Public stock, NPC companies, other towns and cargo never alias that store; Company Value counts physical goods once.
- Pooled capacity is the checked sum of actual granted/paid local facility capacities, including pending construction. Pending sites contribute no output ceiling and skip automatic procurement/production/sales through completion day. Operational same-recipe sites sum their output ceilings, including disabled installed facilities; an unlimited recipe ceiling still respects finite warehouse capacity. Preserve ordinal port/facility execution, fresh shared stock for each policy action, exact material FIFO and successful-batch-only wages. Existing warehouse/industrial-route adapters validate facility ownership and port before resolving the pool; transfers require the actual dock and fresh snapshot.
- Ascension 0 construction stays instant. Ascension 1 newly purchased player sites pay once on day D, complete at the end of D+2 after voyages/before challenge evaluation, and first become production-eligible at completion plus the full interval. Pending sites skip automatic procurement/production/sales even on their completion day. Ordinary owned local warehouse trades/storage and docked transfers remain legal; they cannot trigger early production. Starter grants are already operational. Lifecycle, zero-cash completion events and checked dates share the same atomic rollback.
- Use the snapshot's `PreviewConstructionSchedule` and facility lifecycle properties for UI dates. Do not duplicate timing rules in Unity. Keep industry presets visibly drafted until explicitly applied; report actual cash/material costs and held basis separately from realized margin.
- Archipelago text scaling is presentation-only: Compact/Default/Large/Extra Large use inherited USS layout/text tokens, never a paint transform or economic command. Only the text preference persists via PlayerPrefs; it is not run persistence. Verify all scales at both target resolutions and retain intentional internal scrolling/focus/modal priority.
- ArchipelagoLab launches into the main menu. Pause/results/reference screens stop playback; Escape/NavigationCancel respects modal and confirmation priority. Confirm replacement of unsaved active progress, preserve the exact identity on retry, and distinguish a fresh retry from loading an explicit local save. Use generated encyclopedia format version 2 offline; mechanics in data/player-help.json feed both the game and website. Validate links and freshness, then regenerate rather than hand-edit reference outputs.
- New Run defaults to v3 Grand Commission; Advanced / Legacy retains historical modes and Sandbox remains explicit. Reuse the gameplay ArchipelagoMap for a full-size pre-draft preview; invalid seeds leave the last valid preview intact. Harbor combines public stock, selected hold and actual local company stock with paid trades versus no-cash docked Load/Unload. Use authoritative prices, quantities, production previews and actionable blockers, with explicit legacy-store selection when required. Victory commits/freezes immediately; the host acknowledgment offers results or frozen inspection without another economic step.
- Keep diagnostic export host-owned and development-only. Validate an exact raw save against its report snapshot, create unique bounded files under the host's `persistentDataPath/Exports`, and show their full paths. Export cannot mutate time, identity, stock or the three save slots. Reports separate every real account and material/FIFO owner and label bounded host events as partial history. Never publish raw private playtest saves or infer a complete ledger from retained recent events.
- Price dynamic trades on reversible inventory edges with exact arithmetic and mathematical floor. Preserve same-edge `Ask >= Bid`, bounded positive prices, no profitable closed inventory cycles within one unchanged local pricing model without intervening production, and exact uninterrupted order-splitting totals. Cross-port transport profit is permitted. Preview and execution must share calculations; derive snapshot prices from stock, never a mutable price cache. Do not loop over requested units or distinct prices. Keep `UnitPrice` compatibility semantics explicit: dynamic bulk totals may differ from quantity times first marginal price.
- Document non-data-derived behavior in `docs/MECHANICS.md`. Label design proposals as hypotheses or deferred ideas rather than accepted rules.
- Do not hand-edit `docs/reference/` or `generated/encyclopedia.json`. Regenerate and commit those files with content changes.
- Regenerate `docs/experiments/pricing-results.json` with the `--pricing` demo after relevant experiment/calculation changes; use `--pricing --check` to verify it. Keep authored analysis aligned with measured output and distinguish pricing hypotheses from accounting contracts.
- Regenerate `docs/experiments/archipelago-results.json` with `--archipelago` after scenario or regional behavior changes; verify with `--archipelago --check`. Preserve deterministic reports without machine paths/timestamps, full cash/flow reconciliation, and explicit closed-profit versus held-cargo accounting.
- Generate the Task 1.6 experiment with `--enterprise`; verify with `--enterprise --check`. Preserve historical report bytes when changing only the expanded scenario. Reconcile every company once, markets, ship cargo, private storage, construction transfers, extraction, transformations, and consumption. Choose challenge thresholds from measured merchant and industrial/hybrid trials.
- Generate complete runs with `--runs` and industrial comparisons with `--industrial-logistics`; verify each with `--check`. Preserve deterministic reports, manual economic equivalence, all package/challenge viability and idle-failure cases, exact event/aggregate reconciliation, and honest initial endowment estimates.
- Generate the separate living-city report with `--living-cities`; verify `--living-cities --check`. Keep normal CI bounded and deeper sweeps opt-in. Report actual all-account conservation, physical flows, wages/retail, population/prosperity, stock versus funds shortfalls and provisional pricing comparisons; preserve all historical report bytes. GitHub Pages remains manual-only: do not publish or change settings without authorization.
- V3 is an explicit `ExpandingEconomy` identity: generator/content version 3 and valuation version 1. Preserve exact v1 geography and v2 role draws; never silently reinterpret old modes. Canonical `expandingEconomy` extension is fully validated in both languages, but base catalog enumeration remains six goods; select `ForExpandingEconomy()` explicitly. Generated reference describes both scopes.
- V3 uses 10,000 physical subunits per good and 10,000 monetary subunits per coin with checked int stock/long accounts and wider intermediates. Scale stock targets/capacities/recipe-applied amounts once, keep numeric marginal price ratios, and use shared bounded edge sums. Ship orders require whole cargo-unit multiples; household retail may be fractional. Persist exact current/baseline demand numerator residuals; no rounding away paid goods or split-order penalties.
- Every v3 seed permits all six old private production types plus Silver Mine somewhere, and multiple independently identified eligible sites. Forest furniture cannot build Sawmills. Pay actual extraction/conversion wages; missing inputs/payroll/storage block complete batches. Private Ascension timing stays unchanged.
- Regional money includes markets, households, companies and public treasury; all-account money additionally includes the finite external authority. Check initial regional + minted + external net inflow, and initial all-account + minted at every commit. Only explicit Silver-backed Mint exchanges issue coins. External/public procurement takes real docked cargo with finite funded entitlement; no automatic refill, currency retirement, Bread issuance or household stipend.
- The unique public Royal Mint is commissioned once, activates after its explicit delay and accepts physically transported Silver into a reserve under cadence/lifetime limits. Supplier receipts, public issuance share and surrendered material basis are distinct. Mint/public treasury never become company assets. All development state and failures share the world rollback.
- Shipwright orders consume real local Planks and split actual company payment between market and local labor; completed and pending ships count toward the three-player-ship limit. Each selected owned ship ID targets its own trades, cargo, policy/progress and voyage while sharing company cash once. Keep historical four-action chains, itinerary daily budgets and stable priority/ID daily execution.
- Company Value sums company cash, all distinct owned goods, completed reference ships/sites and paid pending capital once. Starter gifts can have positive reference value with zero paid basis. Never add FIFO basis twice, treat public assets as private, or call reference value/liquidation proceeds/realized margin equivalent. Private wages remain expensed; Mint receipts and public commissions are separate.
- Complete local saves support all four named run modes using a bounded explicit schema, exact identity/content/configuration fingerprints and validated reconstruction, not command replay or runtime type-name deserialization. Preserve fractional residuals, open lots, quotes, all ships, pending projects, public obligations and terminal outcomes without stepping. Simulation owns no paths; host owns safe finite slots, atomic replacement and preserving the active session after invalid load. Retry starts fresh; text preferences remain independent.
- Generate v3 comparisons with `--expanding-economy` and verify `--expanding-economy --check`; verify the separate combined-policy follow-up with `--expanding-economy --reinvestment --check`. Keep deep sweeps opt-in, report actual productive/circulating outcomes and counterexamples, and preserve all historical report bytes. Document actual Editor/player/save journeys separately from headless checks.
- The demo and headless tests use .NET 10; `global.json` selects a stable 10.0 SDK. Keep NuGet lock files and Unity's package manifest/lock synchronized with intentional dependency changes.
- Run `python tools/prepare_unity.py` after simulation, content or player-help changes. It rebuilds and stages the simulation DLL, canonical JSON and current generated encyclopedia under ignored `unity/Assets/Generated/`; never copy simulation source or another Newtonsoft assembly into Unity. Unity's pinned UPM package supplies JSON support.
- Keep TradingLab a UI Toolkit host over the staged simulation DLL. Preserve Bootstrap. Use snapshots, previews, and structured results; never duplicate economic calculations or movement/time rules. Selection, policy editing/application, and enablement are passive. Explicit actions alone step the world; keep activity and multi-tick execution bounded, and distinguish cash changes from closed empty-cargo gross profit. Document controls and recovery checks in `docs/PLAYTEST.md`.
- Keep ArchipelagoLab separate from TradingLab. Its main-thread presentation clock requests whole regional days with bounded catch-up; speed and interpolation cannot change economic calculations. Selection/panel edits are passive. Opening trade/manual orders pauses playback; manual commands require player automation disabled. Derive map positions, NPC visibility, and dock counts from snapshots, and keep recent activity bounded.
- The contextual trade modal shows exact totals and display-only execution averages, uses authoritative maximum quantities and snapshot-bound execution, blocks map input, and supports local/remote/NPC-read-only modes and Escape/focus recovery. Industry/warehouse UI must distinguish public trade from private transfer. Verify both 1366×768 and 1920×1080 in the actual Editor.
- C#: PascalCase for public types/members, camelCase for local variables/parameters, four-space indentation. Python: snake_case, four-space indentation, standard-library solutions when sufficient. Follow adjacent code.
- Add focused tests for meaningful behavior changes and failure cases. Do not scaffold later roadmap features as part of foundation maintenance.
