# Repository guidance

Salt & Ledger supports Expanding Economy v3 with economic rules1, company storage2 and itinerary1. Grand Commission is the primary player run; Sandbox is the unlimited alternative. Classic, seeded-v1 and Living Cities-v2 playable modes, fixed challenges, TradingLab and Bootstrap are retired. Historical compatibility requirements in prior tasks are superseded; Git history is their archive.

Before changing boundaries or behavior read `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/MECHANICS.md`, `docs/EXPANDING_ECONOMY.md`, `docs/LIVING_CITIES.md`, `docs/GRAND_COMMISSION.md`, `docs/HARBOR.md`, `docs/AUTOMATION.md` and `docs/SAVES.md`. For circulation changes also read `docs/ECONOMIC_CIRCULATION_REVIEW.md`.

## Simulation contracts

- Keep `src/SaltAndLedger.Simulation` independent of Unity, presentation, filesystem policy and documentation generation. Preserve netstandard2.1 and C#8. Prefer concrete types and explicit transitions; no speculative service/plugin/quest frameworks.
- Canonical content lives in `data/content.json`, structural schema in `data/content.schema.json`; globally unique stable IDs must not depend on display names. Keep Python/C# validation aligned. Player worlds explicitly use `ForExpandingEconomy()`; base recipe fixtures remain useful low-level tests.
- Preserve exact deterministic modern behavior. No wall-clock time, unseeded randomness, economic repair, hidden replenishment or rebalancing in maintenance. Quantities are nonnegative checked int subunits, cash nonnegative checked long subunits:10,000 per physical good and per coin. Use wider intermediates. Scale recipes/targets/capacities once. Ship orders are whole cargo multiples; local retail/stock can be fractional.
- `RegionalWorldState` is the sole atomic owner of ports, company cash, pools, ships, schedules, households, public development, accounting, objectives, day and revision. Stage all changes then commit once. Exceptional failure preserves all earlier staged work. Instant commands never advance days; rejected/no-op commands preserve snapshot identity. Previews/decisions bind the exact snapshot, not merely its day.
- Daily order: actor priority/ordinal ID; private procurement; due fiscal review; public production by port/priority/ID; private production; private sales; consumption by port/kind/ID; welfare/population and next-day quotes; voyages; due construction; objective evaluation. Shortage is a completed outcome, not an exception. No unbounded world history.
- Company cash is authoritative; ship cash is a projection and never counted twice. Public stock, cargo and each company/town pool are separate owners. `company:<owner>:<port>` is the sole private physical/FIFO store and owns one Buy/Sell policy and trading counters; facilities keep individual enabled/rate/priority/lifecycle/batch/wage counters with empty local inventories and a pool reference.
- Pool capacity is the checked sum of actual local facilities, including paid pending sites. Pending sites contribute no output ceiling and skip production through completion day, but actual paid storage may trade. Operational same-recipe output ceilings sum, including disabled sites. Private execution uses port, High/Normal/Low priority, then ordinal facility ID and fresh shared stock. Only successful full batches pay wages.
- Factory rate0–100 defaults100. Bounded ProductionCredit0–199 admits one whole batch after accumulating100 across scheduled opportunities; rate-skipped deadlines advance, admitted shortages retain credit without accruing another admission. Disabled/rate0 and settings changes preserve phase/deadline. One atomic snapshot-bound command applies enabled/rate/priority. Warehouse rules are independent: buy before production, sell afterward, once per ordinal good, exact paid/FIFO/fee paths. Both directions require BuyTarget<=SellReserve; all known logistics goods allowed. No per-factory trading in pooled worlds.
- Extraction is explicit canonical output; transformations consume exact inputs. Missing input/payroll/storage blocks the whole batch. Due shortages/disabled/output-limited sites remain due without catch-up batches; toggles preserve deadlines. Record extraction, transformation, consumption and transport separately.
- Ascension0 construction is instant. Ascension1 new player sites pay once on D, complete end-of-D+2 after voyages, and first run after one full recipe interval. Starter grants are operational. Manual local stock trades/docked transfers remain legal during construction. UI timing comes from `PreviewConstructionSchedule` and lifecycle properties.
- Trades conserve physical goods and settlement money. Use shared exact reversible inventory-edge pricing and mathematical floor; Ask>=Bid on the same edge, bounded positive prices, no profitable unchanged-local closed cycles and exact uninterrupted splitting totals. Do not loop over requested units/distinct prices. Preview/execution share calculations; bulk total need not equal first marginal price times quantity.
- FIFO slices retain original intervals: floor(C*end/Q)-floor(C*start/Q). Transfers move original slices without new basis; production allocates consumed material basis across ordinal outputs. Wages/service expenses remain separate, never silently capitalized. Starter grants have zero paid basis; construction is capital. Company Value counts cash, distinct owned goods, completed reference ships/sites and paid pending capital once, excluding public assets and duplicate FIFO basis.
- Household cash is a separate real town account. Successful public/private employers pay wages; paid household retail pays the market, essential before luxury, preserving exact demand residuals and separate stock/funds shortfalls. Municipal own-use pays nobody. Keep six bounded food observations, slow population/prosperity reviews and immutable daily Coverage parameters. Never create money from Bread or add a stipend.
- Regional money equals initial regional + minted + external net inflow; all-account money equals initial all-account + minted. Only the commissioned public Royal Mint issues currency against physically delivered Silver under cadence/lifetime limits. Supplier receipts, public share and surrendered material basis are distinct. Contracts/public procurement consume real docked cargo and finite funded entitlement.
- Circulation1 ordinary company sales charge2% from gross proceeds, including NPCs and local stock. Per-company/town cumulative floor prefixes preserve split totals; half cumulative fees go to market, remainder households. Gross affordability/revenue/material FIFO remain unchanged. One bounded four-day levy/relief review protects actual ready payroll/next-food reserves; no food means no relief, no feasible work means no payroll levy. No wealth cap or hidden bailout.
- Shipwright orders consume real local Planks, pay market and labor, and count pending/completed ships toward the three-player-ship limit. Commands target each selected ship while sharing company cash once. Departure costs no day; each day advances voyages once, and committed arrival permits immediate dockside trades. Remote previews never allow remote cargo movement.
- Player itineraries:2–4 unique connected stops,1–8 commands per stop,24 total; each Buy/Sell/Load/Unload executes its largest qualifying whole-cargo quantity once per visit. Below minimum Skip advances without movement/payment; Wait retains the exact command. Preserve mixed/unsold cargo. Cursor/cycle/last reason/OperationsDay/Used are authoritative; eight operations per ship/day include successful orders, skips and departure. Repeated calls/save/load cannot reset the budget.
- Route replacement requires manual control, an included actual dock, no partial old stop and no operations today. Equivalent reapplication preserves progress. Pause retains voyage/cargo/cursor; enablement validates the next dock/incoming leg. Policies are mutually exclusive. Presets draft routes without building/funding anything.
- Keep `SeededArchipelago` pure geography and `LivingCityGeneration` role draws: v3 composes them. These are required shared algorithms, not selectable old modes. Every seed offers current production types somewhere, including multiple private sites; forest furniture cannot build Sawmills. NPC market-route projection/evaluator and controlled-research industrial policies remain required. Do not rewrite them solely to erase an old class name.
- Run identity retains seed/generator3/content3/valuation1, pricing, charter, Ascension, commission draft/signature, storage2/itinerary1/economic1. Grand Commission has four salted category streams, three of four templates offered each, one choice per round. All four goals and 100 liquid coins by day 180 are mandatory, exact-deadline success first. Terminal states freeze mutation.
- Visits/net sales are bounded authoritative history, credited only from actual docks/arrivals and company market trades. Buyback reverses net supply. A New Industry requires the same funded operational Mill/Bakery pair in distinct towns. Current conditions and cumulative accomplishments differ. Card progress is its minimum component; objective score=floor(500*sum(four percentages)/400).
- Save schema6 only, including factory rate/priority/credit and warehouse policy/counters. Reject old schemas, wrong identities, content/configuration/checksum mismatch, impossible routes/history/accounts or duplicate physical/FIFO holdings before replacing the active session. Use explicit bounded fields, no runtime-type deserialization/replay. Host owns three safe slots, atomic writes and error recovery; simulation owns no paths. Retry starts fresh, Load resumes exact state. Diagnostic economy0/treatment worlds cannot masquerade as normal saves.

## Host and documentation

- ArchipelagoLab is the sole scene/build host and starts at the main menu. UI Toolkit consumes snapshots/previews/results without duplicating economics. Current map, Harbor, Industry, Fleet, Economy, Objectives and encyclopedia remain functional. Selection/navigation/drafts/scale changes are passive. Playback requests bounded whole days; rendering/interpolation never changes outcomes.
- Pause, Journal, reference and results stop playback. Escape/NavigationCancel respects modal priority. Confirm unsaved replacement. Trade modals block map input and use authoritative quantities/fees/stale-snapshot checks. Harbor distinguishes paid market trades from no-cash actual-dock transfers. No placeholder future systems.
- Compact/Default/Large/Extra Large use inherited layout/text tokens, not paint scaling. Only this preference persists in PlayerPrefs. Verify1366×768 and 1920×1080 with actual Editor/player interaction when changing UI.
- Diagnostic exports are host-owned/development-only, bounded unique files under persistentDataPath/Exports, with exact raw save/report agreement. They never mutate time or slots. Recent events are partial history. Never publish private raw saves.
- `data/player-help.json` feeds both website and in-game encyclopedia format2. Do not hand-edit `docs/reference/` or `generated/encyclopedia.json`; regenerate and check links/freshness. Update exact non-content mechanics in docs/MECHANICS.md. Label deferred ideas as proposals. Do not publish or alter GitHub Pages without authorization.
- Use .NET10 stable SDK/locked NuGet; keep package manifests/locks synchronized. Run `python tools/prepare_unity.py` after simulation/content/help edits. It stages DLL/content/help under ignored Unity Generated; never copy simulation source or another Newtonsoft assembly into Unity.
- Add scenario/invariant tests for meaningful changes and failure cases. Remove retired-only/redundant tests; preserve money/physical/FIFO/atomicity/pricing/generation/current-save/current-objective protections. Current reports must remain deterministic and reconcile every owner once. Deep sweeps stay opt-in.
- C#: PascalCase public members, camelCase locals, four spaces. Python: snake_case/four spaces and standard library where sufficient. Follow adjacent code.
