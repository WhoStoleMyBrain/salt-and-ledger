## Contracts under verification

- V3 selects `RunWorldMode.ExpandingEconomy`, generator/content version 3 and valuation version 1. It shares the existing regional owner, exact transaction/pricing/production operations and material FIFO. Default content loading remains the historical six-good catalog; `ForExpandingEconomy()` selects the canonical scoped Silver/Mine/Mint extension.
- Physical goods and currency both use 10,000 integer subunits per displayed unit. Ship orders are whole cargo multiples; retail and local warehouse transactions may be fractional. Production executes complete scaled canonical recipes. Proportional demand carries exact residuals; display formatting never feeds economics.
- Regional money includes markets, households, companies and treasury once. The finite external authority is outside the region. Regional cash changes by recorded Mint issuance plus external inflow; all-account cash changes only by issuance. Bread/Furniture purchases transfer existing money and consume real goods.
- Public Mint commission is 400 coins, four days. One physically delivered Silver issues 80 coins split 60 supplier/20 treasury, with a four-day interval and 24-batch lifetime authorization. Public property never enters private Company Value.
- Ship commissioning costs 500 coins and four actual market Planks; payment splits 460 market/40 household labor. Delivery is D+4, empty at the shipwright. Completed and pending ships together have a three-ship cap.
- Company Value counts cash, all owned physical goods, completed ships/sites and paid pending capital exactly once, at stable reference values. Grants have zero historical cost but positive reference value. Material margin, wages, issuance receipts, capital spending and reference-value change remain distinct.
- The filesystem-free save codec reconstructs bounded, validated authoritative state. The host owns three local slots and atomic file replacement. Loading validates before replacing the active session and performs no economic step. Retry recreates the original identity instead of loading a save.

### Completed reinvestment comparison

The separate [reinvestment report](experiments/expanding-reinvestment-results.json) compares twelve worlds over 365 days: seeds -42/42/99, both pricing modes and both ascensions. Its unchanged-rule policy commissions the finite Mint, adds real upstream/downstream employers and three ships, then physically returns unused Silver to storage and changes that ship to food/input commerce. All twelve worlds issue the same bounded 1920 coins; the comparison does not add income or stock to obtain a result.

At day 365, **StockOnly seeds 42 and 99 under both ascensions have 100% current paid-food windows in their processing towns**, with populations 34-46 above their initial 20/30. The vulnerable recovery targets remain partly supplied: 56/54% for seed 42 and 41/38% for seed 99. Seed 42/A0 produced 156 private Flour and 380 Bread, paid 3758 wages and retained 356.1313 company cash; seed 99/A0 produced 166 Flour/398 Bread, paid 3848 wages and retained 1265.9018. All six Coverage cases have zero food in all towns at day 365. A current six-observation window does not imply uninterrupted full welfare throughout the year.

The optional twelve-world 1095-day follow-up eventually reaches zero food and population 10 everywhere. In seed 99/StockOnly/A0, the region's 12850 coins remain exactly accounted: NPC companies 8483.6664, player 100.0282, markets 3436.3012, treasury 830 and households 0.0042.
